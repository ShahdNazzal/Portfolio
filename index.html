01 About

I like making things that work — and understanding why they do.

I just finished my degree in Artificial Intelligence and Data Science at Yarmouk University, and I spent those four years building rather than only studying: a driving-test system that watches the road, a RAG assistant for company knowledge, a fitness platform with an AI coach inside it, and a churn analysis taken from raw data to dashboards.

My interests are deliberately wide — models, data, backends, interfaces — and I’m at my best where they meet. I learn fast and I finish what I start.
